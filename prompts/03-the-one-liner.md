# Chapter 3: The one-line prompt (baseline)

The before picture. Send a one-line prompt with the rules in place and see what you get with no brief, no assets and no loop.

## Prompt

```text
make a dynamic 15-second motion graphics video that shows what an incredible motion designer you are, like it's your showreel for a résumé. go all out.
```

## Variant (the very first test, sent before the studio or rules existed)

```text
Make a 10-second motion graphics video of a weather app forecast: the sun rises, clouds roll in, it starts raining, and the temperature ticks down.
```
