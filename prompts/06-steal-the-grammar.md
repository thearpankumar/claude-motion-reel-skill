# Chapter 6: Steal the grammar, not the content

Learn a reference film's rhythm, transitions, camera and type, write a style guide and shotlist in that style, then wait for approval.

## Prompt

```text
Reference: https://x.com/javiiarchive/status/2103897550449516644 (the mtioon launch film, 15s, 16:9)

1. Download the video with yt-dlp into ./refs/, then extract one frame every 0.5s with ffmpeg into ./refs/frames/. Study them.
2. Write ./docs/style_guide.md: palette (hex), type (family, weight, tracking),
   shot lengths, transition types, camera moves, texture/grain, how text enters and exits.
3. Write ./docs/shotlist.md for a 15s 16:9 launch video about MagicPath (https://magicpath.ai) in THAT style.
   Use real MagicPath screenshots and logo from the site. Take the grammar of the reference, never its content, logos or characters.
4. Show me both files. Wait for my OK before any code.
```

## Make it yours
Any launch film you admire works as the reference. Reply `ok` once the style guide and shotlist look right.
