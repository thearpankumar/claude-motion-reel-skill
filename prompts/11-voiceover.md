# Chapter 11: Add a voiceover

The structured brief plus a Fish Audio voiceover synced to the cuts, with the follow-up that fixed it.

## Prompt

```text
Make a dynamic 20-second motion graphics video for MagicPath (https://magicpath.ai), with the energy
of a motion designer's showreel. Go all out.

Assets
- Visit the site. Use real screenshots (Playwright), the real logo, real colors and fonts.
  Save everything to ./assets and list what you found before you animate.
- Never redraw the product UI from imagination. Crop and animate the real thing.

Story (one beat each, 2 to 4 seconds)
1. Hook: the problem in 5 words of huge kinetic type: "One developer. One chat. Slow."
2. The product appears: "The shared workspace for humans and agents." The canvas assembles itself piece by piece.
3. Three features, each as a UI moment with a cursor doing a real action:
   a. One prompt, a team of designer agents, multiple screens appearing on the canvas at the same time.
   b. Claude Code, Codex or Cursor plugged in and building directly on the canvas.
   c. Design becomes code: a canvas component pushed into the repo as real React.
4. One number that proves it works: 5 designer agents building 5 screens at once.
5. Logo lockup + "Path 2.0. Try it at magicpath.ai"

Sound
- Original music, 120 BPM, synthesized in code. UI clicks and whooshes on the beat.
- Voiceover: use the Fish Audio MCP to generate a short, confident voiceover for each beat and sync it to the cuts. Keep the music under it.

Format: 1920x1080 (16:9) first, then 1:1 and 9:16 from the same timeline.
Before the full render, show me a contact sheet of one frame per beat.
```

## Follow-up

```text
use my voice... and dont say things like "slow",, only say the things that make sense to say
```

## Make it yours
The second message is the follow-up that fixed it: narrate in your own cloned voice, and never read the gag captions aloud.
